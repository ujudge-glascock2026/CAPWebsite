This was my first computer science project. I grew from having never coded in my life, to creating a prototype of a music theory learning app. I learned how to translate my app’s function into directly function steps, through inputs and outputs. It was most difficult for me to create outputs that accounted for every possible input, but I learned to plan ahead. I focused on isolating the individual input-output relationships, to simplify the complexities of my app.
