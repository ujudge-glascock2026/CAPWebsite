This is an example of my best work, because of the detail I put into the turtle drawing. I am proud of my use of both perpendicular and arc lines to create a dynamic image that represents a reptile balancing a ball. Although it was difficult to adjust the arc lines to create organic shapes, through trial and error, I was able to persevere.
